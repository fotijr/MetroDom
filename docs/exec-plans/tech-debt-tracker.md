| TD-23 | M | `src/MetroDom.Conductor/Midi/Identifier.cs` | Discovery steps 1-4 on CoreMIDI only. | TD-26 | M | `src/MetroDom.Core/Timing/BeatTracker.cs`, `BeatFollower.cs`, `metrodom play --follow` | Tuned and tested on modelled human timings only: no real drummer recordings yet (need M5 triggers, then `beats --record`).
